About Victory Sports Arena

Our Story

Victory Sports Arena is a sports facility in North Halishahar, Chattogram, providing a dedicated environment for football, cricket, futsal and competitive sports activities. The arena has hosted tournaments and sports events and has invested in upgraded turf infrastructure.
