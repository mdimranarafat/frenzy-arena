About Frenzy

Two ways to play

Frenzy Sports Arena brings artificial futsal turf and a swimming pool together on one site in Saraipara, Pahartali — beside Joy Nagar Jame Masjid, opposite the north Greenview gate.

Whether it's a five-a-side match with your crew or laps in the pool, Frenzy keeps its doors open around the clock so you can play on your own schedule.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a slot at Frenzy?

Use the "Request a Slot" form on this page, call +880 1881-271887, or message the Facebook page. Frenzy will confirm your turf or pool slot directly.

What are Frenzy's opening hours?

Frenzy is always open — call ahead to confirm the best time to come in, especially for peak evening slots.

Where exactly is Frenzy located?

On Port Connecting Road in Saraipara, Pahartali — beside Joy Nagar Jame Masjid and opposite the north Greenview gate.

Can I book for a group or team event?

Yes — call or message ahead to arrange a slot for your team or a group swim session.

Do you have both futsal and swimming available the same day?

Yes, both facilities operate on site — mention what you're looking to book when you call.
